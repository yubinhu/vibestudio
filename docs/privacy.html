VibeStudio

Privacy Policy

Effective September 19, 2026 · Applies to the VibeStudio desktop and iOS apps

The short version: VibeStudio does not collect your workspace content or usage data. There are no VibeStudio accounts, analytics, or tracking. Your devices connect directly to your own computers without a VibeStudio relay. Downloads and optional integrations contact third-party services as described below.

What we collect

The apps have no VibeStudio accounts to sign up for, contain no analytics or tracking code, and do not collect or transmit personal data, usage data, or workspace content to us or to anyone on our behalf. Information you choose to send for support and Apple's opt-in diagnostics are described below.

SSH keys and managed secrets

The iOS app generates SSH key pairs on your device. The private key is stored in the iOS Keychain, with an app-private file fallback if the native store cannot initialize. It never leaves your device — it is not backed up to us, synced through us, or visible to us in any way. Only the public key is shown so you can install it on your own computers.

Secrets you manage for coding agents are stored on the active computer in local JSON and shell environment files. On macOS and Linux, these files are restricted to your user account. Agents load these values into their environment when needed; VibeStudio does not store them in the macOS Keychain or send them to us.

Where your data goes

The iOS app connects directly to computers you own and control over SSH. Desktop clients use the local host or a selected SSH or WSL connection. We have no relay in the middle of these workspace connections and no access to their contents.

Browser phone access uses HTTPS to your host through your Tailscale network. This path depends on your Tailscale configuration and access controls; it does not use a VibeStudio relay.

Agents you run, GitHub publishing or synchronization, and connector services may send data to their respective providers according to your configuration and actions. For example, a cloud-backed coding agent may send prompts and code to its model provider. Those services operate under their own accounts and privacy policies; their traffic does not pass through VibeStudio-operated servers.

If you enable browser push notifications, your host sends encrypted notification payloads through the browser's push provider. A notification can include the session label and a preview of terminal output. VibeStudio does not operate that push service. Native local notifications are generated on your device.

Updates and downloads

VibeStudio checks GitHub Releases for updates and downloads application or server packages when needed. The optional on-device model uses model files downloaded from Hugging Face; model inference then runs on the active computer. These downloads do not upload workspace files, but ordinary HTTPS request information, such as the requesting IP address, is visible to the download provider. GitHub requests are covered by GitHub's privacy statement, and model downloads by Hugging Face's privacy policy.

Crash reports and support

VibeStudio does not automatically send us diagnostic logs. We may receive TestFlight feedback and App Store crash reports through Apple's opt-in mechanisms, controlled by your settings with Apple. We also receive information you choose to include in an email, issue, or other support request.

Children

The apps' no-analytics and no-tracking commitments apply equally to everyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the apps ever change in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and change the effective date at the top.
